About

A digital agency that also runs the commerce side, because handing it back half-finished never worked.

Lead Strategy is a Canadian company built around one observation: most small businesses do not need more website. They need the part of it that brings work in to actually function — and someone to keep running it after launch day.

Lead Strategy Canada Inc.Founded 2024Canada-wide, remote-first

Why we exist

The usual arrangement fails in a predictable way. An agency builds a site, invoices, and leaves. Six months later the plugins are out of date, the contact form is delivering to a mailbox nobody reads, and the ad account is owned by a contractor who has stopped replying. Nothing looks broken, so nobody notices — and the business quietly stops receiving enquiries it paid for.

We built the four divisions so that gap has nowhere to open. Build makes the thing. Market brings people to it. Create supplies the material both need. Sell runs the commercial side once it is live. One team, one number to call, and no handoff where accountability disappears.

How we work

Six commitments, in writing
01

You own everything

Code, design files, raw footage, ad accounts, analytics, domains. Handover is a checklist, not a negotiation, and it happens whether you stay or leave.

02

Scoped before it starts

A written document with what gets built, what it costs, and what happens if the shape changes. No open-ended hourly drift.

03

Phased so each step pays for the next

The piece that earns fastest goes first. Stop after any phase and you still own something that works on its own.

04

Measured in revenue

One agreed number per engagement — enquiries, orders, gifts or bookings. Not impressions, not sessions, not a vanity dashboard.

05

We say no out loud

If a division is not the right fit, or a merch idea will not cover its own costs, you hear it before you spend rather than after.

06

No tricks that age badly

No link schemes, no dark-pattern checkouts, no forced account creation. They work briefly and then they cost you.
