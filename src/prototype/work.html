How we agree to be measured

Before a build starts we pick one number that decides whether it worked. Not traffic. Not impressions. Usually enquiries, orders, gifts or bookings — the thing your business counts anyway.

If that number does not move, we say so in the monthly note rather than reporting around it. Sometimes the honest answer is that the site was never the constraint.

Agreed up front

One measure per engagement

Written into the scope document alongside the price, so nobody has to relitigate what success meant six months later.

Reported monthly

In revenue, not impressions

One dashboard with the numbers you actually check: orders, revenue, cost per order, and where buyers came from.
