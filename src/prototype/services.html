How an engagement runs

Same four steps, every division
Step one

Scope in writing

A fixed document listing what gets built, what it costs, what we need from you, and what happens if the shape changes. Nothing starts before you have it.

Step two

Phase it so each step pays for the next

The piece that earns the fastest goes first. You should be able to stop after any phase and still own something that works.

Step three

A link every week

Staging is open from day one. You watch it get built instead of waiting for a reveal, and course corrections cost hours rather than weeks.

Step four

Handover, properly

Code, design files, raw footage, ad accounts, analytics and documentation transfer to you. If you leave, nothing breaks and nothing is held back.
